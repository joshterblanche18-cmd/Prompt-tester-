10. Costs and money: our monthly costs, payment fees and income at 10, 20 and 50 clients, split per partner. Run the arithmetic in code and show it.
11. Risks: legal, platform (app store rules), payment and reputation risks, each with a workaround and its cost.
12. Portfolio capture: what proof to collect at each stage (screenshots, short videos, results with client permission) so this project becomes a case study for our studio.
13. Next 3 actions: the very next things to do this week, smallest first.
