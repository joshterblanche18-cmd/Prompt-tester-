    How rank points work

    • Consistency, about 60%. 10 per session, 25 for hitting your weekly goal, 50 for every 4 weeks in a row.
    • Strength, about 30%. Your squat, bench and deadlift total divided by body weight, scored only against people in your weight class.
    • Personal bests, about 10%. 40 each time you beat your own best, verified by a coach.

    Weight classes. Women: 57, 63, 72, 84 and 84+ kg. Men: 66, 74, 83, 93, 105 and 105+ kg. A coach does a weigh-in once a month. Anyone can pick Open instead, which ranks on consistency only.
